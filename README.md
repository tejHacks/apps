## How to install

1. Tap the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager ("Install unknown apps").
3. Tap **Install**.

These are test builds, not Play Store releases. Each one installs, opens and runs on an Android emulator before it is published here.

## AidNow: not medical advice

AidNow gives general first-aid guidance. It is not a medical device and not a substitute for professional medical help or a first-aid course. Its content has not yet been reviewed by a clinician. **In an emergency, always call your local emergency number first.**
